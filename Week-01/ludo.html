    How to play

    Be the first to move all four of your tokens around the board and into your home triangle.

    Taking turns

    • Roll the die and move one of your tokens that many squares, clockwise around the board.
    • Every token starts in its base. You need to roll a 6 to bring a token out onto your colored start square.
    • Rolling a 6 gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row ends your turn.
    • After going around the board, a token turns into your colored home column. It needs the exact number to reach home, and bringing a token home earns another roll.

    Capturing

    • If your token lands on a square holding an opponent's token, that token is captured and sent back to its base. Every opponent token on that square is captured.
    • A captured token must roll a 6 again to re-enter the board, so it loses all its progress.
    • Capturing earns you another roll.
    • Safe squares (the stars and the colored start squares) can't be used for captures. Any number of tokens can share them.
    • Your own tokens never capture each other, and tokens in a home column are always safe.
    • Computer players hunt for captures too, so think twice before parking just in front of one.

    Number of players

    • 2 players: you play from opposite corners (top-left and bottom-right). The two unused corners and their colors are removed from the board. Turns simply alternate, and the first player to get all four tokens home wins.
    • 3 players: the top-left, top-right and bottom-right corners play, and the bottom-left corner is removed.
    • 4 players: everyone plays for themselves, or turn on team mode to play 2 vs 2 with the opposite corner as your partner.

    Controls

    • Click the die, or press Space, to roll.
    • Click a highlighted token, or press 1–4, to move it. If only one move is possible, it's made for you.
