# Week 01

Materials for Week 1 of GBUS 562: AI Language Models.

## Contents

- [connect-four.html](connect-four.html): a single-page Connect Four game. Play against a computer opponent (Easy, Medium or Hard), or switch to 2 Players to take turns with a friend on the same device. Download the file and open it in any web browser to play.
- [ludo.html](ludo.html): a single-page Ludo game for 2, 3 or 4 players, human or computer (Easy, Medium or Hard). Two-player games use opposite corners, and unused corners are removed from the board. It follows traditional capture rules: landing on an opponent's token sends it back to its base, where it needs a 6 to re-enter, and the star and start squares are safe. It also includes an optional 2 vs 2 team mode, custom player colors, sound effects, animated moves, save and load, game statistics, a winner celebration and a built-in "How to play" guide. Download the file and open it in any web browser to play.
