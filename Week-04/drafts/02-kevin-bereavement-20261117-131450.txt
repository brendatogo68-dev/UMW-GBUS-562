Hi Kevin,

I'm so sorry to hear about the loss of your grandmother.

Under our Bereavement and Family Leave policy, for the death of a grandparent you're entitled to up to 3 paid days off. These days don't need to be taken consecutively, but they do need to be used within 60 days. This time is paid and doesn't reduce your PTO or sick balance. You don't need to provide documentation — just let your supervisor know as soon as you can so they can plan around your time off. (Source: bereavement-and-family-leave.html)

Please let your supervisor know about the dates you'll need, including Saturday for the funeral in Albuquerque and any travel time you may need around it.

Wishing you and your family comfort during this difficult time.
