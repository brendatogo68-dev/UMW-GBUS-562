ANSWER
The overdraft fee is $29 per item paid, with no more than three overdraft or returned item fees charged to one account in a single business day. No overdraft fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10 or less at the end of the business day [consumer-fee-schedule.html].

Note: This is the current fee schedule (Version 8). An earlier version (Version 7) lists the fee as $35 per item with a limit of four per day, but Version 8 replaces it [consumer-fee-schedule.html][consumer-fee-schedule-v7.html].
