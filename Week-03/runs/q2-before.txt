ANSWER
A $2,000 paycheck deposited by direct deposit (payroll) is available on the business day it is received, so the full $2,000 would be available that day [funds-availability-policy.html]. However, if this was deposited as a physical check at a branch rather than direct deposit, only the first $275 is available on the next business day, with the remainder available on the second business day after deposit [funds-availability-policy.html].
