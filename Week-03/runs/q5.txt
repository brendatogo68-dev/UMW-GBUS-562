ANSWER
Only **$275** of the $2,000 paycheck is available on the next business day; the remaining $1,725 becomes available on the second business day after the deposit. This is because a paycheck deposited at a branch is treated as a check deposit, not a direct deposit — the check deposit rule caps next-day availability at $275, with the rest available the second business day [funds-availability-policy.html].

(Note: if the paycheck had instead been received as a direct deposit, e.g., payroll deposited electronically, the full amount would be available the same business day [funds-availability-policy.html] — but since this was a paper check deposited at a branch, the check deposit rule applies.)
